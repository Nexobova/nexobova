What is Nexobova?

Nexobova (also known as Nexobova HQ) is the Revenue Upgrade Architect: a business software and digital solutions company in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. We design, build and run AI solutions, websites and apps, digital marketing and business automation — including CRM, GST billing, ERP, POS, HR and WhatsApp automation — for businesses across India and worldwide.

Is Nexobova the same as Nexonova?

No. Nexobova — spelled N-E-X-O-B-O-V-A — is a Bangalore software and AI company known as the Revenue Upgrade Architect, at www.nexobova.com. It is not related to Nexonova, Nexo Nova or any similarly named company.

What does “Revenue Upgrade Architect” mean?

We start from your revenue goals, not from a software list. Our team maps how your business wins and serves customers today, then designs and builds the website, marketing, automation and AI systems that grow revenue — with scope, timeline and price agreed in writing.

Which websites has Nexobova built recently?

Recent launches include the Veltrix Engineering website (veltrixengineering.in), a Bangalore home renovation and interiors company, and the Colours K corporate apparel catalogue (coloursk.com). Earlier work includes Ninnu Enterprises, Olive Dental, JPS Group, Nila Sol and JWJTP EduOrg.

What AI solutions does Nexobova build?

Nine AI agents and tools: AI Chatbot Builder, AI Content Studio, AI Document Reader, AI Sales Assistant, AI Voice Agent, AI Video Content Agent, AI Social Media Agent, AI AutoPilot for RPA and workflow automation, and AI SAP Data Studio for enterprise master-data cleansing, reconciliation and BI reporting.

Does Nexobova clean SAP data and reports for enterprises?

Yes. For large companies we cleanse and govern SAP S/4HANA and ECC master data — duplicate vendors, customers, materials and business partners — validate GL and cost-centre records with AI, automate reconciliation, and deliver audit-ready BI and KPI reports.
