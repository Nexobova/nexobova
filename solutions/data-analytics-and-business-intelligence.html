What is included in Nexobova Data, Analytics & Business Intelligence?

Data, Analytics & Business Intelligence covers BI Dashboards, Data Engineering & ETL, Forecasting & Predictive Analytics, Automated MIS Reporting, AI SAP Data Studio, Data Cleansing & Governance, Data Warehousing and KPI Design & Scorecards. On a free scoping call we recommend only what your business needs.

How is it priced?

Every project is quoted after a free scoping call, with fixed scope, timeline and price agreed in writing before work starts. Ongoing services are billed monthly.

Do you work outside Bangalore?

Yes. Our team is in JP Nagar, Bangalore, and we work with businesses across India and worldwide — on-site where needed and remotely everywhere else.
