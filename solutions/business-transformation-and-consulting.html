FAQ

Questions about Business Transformation & Consulting

What is included in Nexobova Business Transformation & Consulting?

Business Transformation & Consulting covers Revenue Strategy, Digital Transformation Roadmap, Process Redesign, CTO-as-a-Service for Startups, Technology Selection, Business Health Checkup, Change Management & Training and Project & Programme Management. On a free scoping call we recommend only what your business needs.

How is it priced?

Every project is quoted after a free scoping call, with fixed scope, timeline and price agreed in writing before work starts. Ongoing services are billed monthly.

Which industries does Nexobova serve with Business Transformation & Consulting?

We deliver Business Transformation & Consulting for startups & new businesses, msmes, enterprise, innovation & tech ventures, manufacturing, retail and many more of the 100 industries we serve.

Do you work outside Bangalore?

Yes. Our team is in JP Nagar, Bangalore, and we work with businesses across India and worldwide — on-site where needed and remotely everywhere else.
