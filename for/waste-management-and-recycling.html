FAQ

Which Nexobova apps suit waste management & recycling?

Most waste management & recycling customers start with Fleet Manager and Mobile App, then add Inventory, Billing & GST, Custom Web App as they grow. Our team recommends the right mix on a free scoping call.

Can you move our existing data?

Yes. We migrate data from Excel, Tally or your current software as part of setup, and train your staff before go-live.

Yes. Every Nexobova app supports multiple branches with role-based access and owner dashboards across locations.
