Questions about Tours & Travel Desk

Is Tours & Travel Desk set up for me?

Yes. Our team configures Tours & Travel Desk for your workflows, moves your existing data from Excel, Tally or other software, and trains your staff before go-live.

Does Tours & Travel Desk work with other Nexobova apps?

Yes. Tours & Travel Desk shares one login and one data layer with every Nexobova app, so customers, invoices, stock and reports stay in sync.
